My Personal Action

Here I share my own family's massive transition towards sustainable transport choices — the financial shifts, the major lifestyle changes, and our long-term green mobility goals.

🚗
The Starting Point

Moving Away From Heavy Vehicle Reliance

My family used to rely heavily on our 4 gasoline-powered cars for almost daily use, including sending the children to school, our work commutes, and running errands like supermarket trips. This lifestyle meant that my family alone spent over $800 a month on fuel alone. Beyond the steep financial cost, this heavy reliance contributed significantly to our local carbon footprint, increasing greenhouse gas emissions in our community.

💰 Financial Impact: Over $800/month spent just on gasoline.
🍃
The Big Step

Selling the Cars & Embracing a New Way to Move

Recognizing the environmental and financial toll, we made a radical change: we sold our cars. Making the transition away from personal vehicle ownership has forced us to completely rethink how we move, pushing us to embrace alternative transportation options that are far kinder to the planet.

🌱 What we learned: Breaking a habit as big as car ownership takes courage but makes an immediate impact.
