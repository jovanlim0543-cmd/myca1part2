About Me

Jovan Lim

Student · Climate Advocate · Sustainable Transport Enthusiast

Hello! I am a Computer science student that cares much about the environment and wants to raise awareness about how each individual can play a big part in taking ownership of the environment. Grew up with a passion to change the world, inspired by Martin Kochman, I enjoy studying how to delay climate change.

I believe that sustainable transport isn't just an environmental issue its where people would genuinely feel comfortable to travel in while giving their best to a greener place. From cycling lanes to public transit, the decisions we make about movement define our shared future.

You'll find me cycling around the neighbourhood or exploring new MRT lines in my free time!.

My Mission Statement

This project aims to make sustainable transport feel accessible and inspiring. I want to show that change does not require perfection; it requires the heart, informed choices and the courage to impact others for better systems.

Transport in this world shapes everything: air quality, public health, economic, and climate change. By understanding our options, its impacts and our aim, each of us play a part in one of the most important transitions of our generation.

Thank you for your time. I hope this site motivates you to take one small step or pedal to a greener future.
